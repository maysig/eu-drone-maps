European Drone Maps

Each country's aviation authority publishes its own map of UAS geographical zones. This page links to every one of them. Nothing is copied, so each map opens at its official source and is always current.

    About the sources

    Verified means the link comes from the country's own official body: its aviation authority, air navigation provider or transport ministry. Unverified means it comes from a third-party list and no official confirmation was found. An unverified map may well be correct. Open it with care and check the country's official page through EASA's list of national aviation authorities.

    Iceland has no online map. Last checked 2 November 2026. Rules differ by country and this page does not replace official information.
